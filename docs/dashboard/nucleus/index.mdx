Nucleus is, at its core, a secure reverse proxy provided by Cfx.re for your FiveM server.
It gives your server a license-key-linked web address that can be used, and was planned, for a plethora of things.
You can read more about it in the [official forum post](https://forum.cfx.re/t/web-administration-and-cfx-re-project-nucleus/676286) announcing it.
Unfortunately, 7 years later, after a whole ecosystem was built around it, Cfx.re [deprecated the Nucleus links](https://forum.cfx.re/t/nucleus-reverse-proxy-deprecation/5387399) on June 30, 2026.

To keep supporting this ecosystem we offer a free alternative in the Qbox Dashboard. Nucleus is free on every plan.

## Setup

Open the **Nucleus** page. On your first visit it shows **Set up the Nucleus proxy**. Fill in the **Server details**:

- **Server IP**: the public IP address of your FiveM server. Use a raw IPv4 or IPv6 address; hostnames are not accepted.
- **Server port**: the port FXServer listens on, 30120 by default.

Not sure which port your server uses? Check your `server.cfg`:
```bash
endpoint_add_tcp "0.0.0.0:30120"
endpoint_add_udp "0.0.0.0:30120"
```
![nucleus fill in form](https://r2.qbox.re/chatty/docs/nucleus-form.png)

Press **Create the proxy**. The **Proxy URL** card then shows your **Public URL**, the **Server config** line, and the **Connect URL**.
Copy the **Server config** line into your `server.cfg`, for example:
```bash
set web_baseUrl "chatty.server.qbox.re"
```
![nucleus dashboard](https://r2.qbox.re/chatty/docs/nucleus.png)

Now existing resources like [lb-upload](https://github.com/lbphone/lb-upload) will keep working and serve content through the `*.server.qbox.re` provided url.

## Changing the server address

If your server moves to a new IP or port, enter it in the **Upstream server** card and press **Save upstream**.
Saving has a short cooldown. If you save too often, the button shows **Please wait** with a countdown.

## Custom domain

You can serve the proxy from your own hostname, such as `connect.example.com`, instead of the `server.qbox.re` one.

1. At your DNS provider, add a **CNAME** record for your hostname that points at `server.qbox.re`. If your DNS is on Cloudflare, set the record to **DNS only** (grey cloud).
2. In the **Custom domain** card, enter the hostname in **Custom hostname**. Enter only the hostname, without `https://`, a port, or a path.
3. Press **Save domain**. The dashboard shows the DNS record to add, in case you have not done it yet.
4. Wait for the status next to **Managed hostname** to change to **Active**. This usually takes a few minutes.
5. Once it is active, the **Server config** line switches to your domain. Copy the new `set web_baseUrl` line into your `server.cfg`.

To remove your domain, clear **Custom hostname** and press **Save domain**.

## Deleting the proxy

Press **Delete proxy** in the **Danger zone** card. This removes your proxy URL and all its routes, so your server is no longer reachable through it. It cannot be undone.
