# Questionnaires

A questionnaire is a simple form for feedback or votes: "What should we add next?", "How was the last event?", "Which car pack do you want?".
It uses the same question types as an application form, but there is nothing to review: no staff queue, no Discord post, no roles handed out. Players answer once and you read the results.

Questionnaires live on the **Applications** page, so they need a plan that includes applications (see [Plans](/dashboard/applications#plans)). Only people with **Applications** set to **Manage** can create them, see the **Questionnaires** tab, and read the results.

## Create a questionnaire

1. Go to **Applications** → **Questionnaires** and press **New questionnaire**.
2. Type a **Name**.
3. Decide on **Anonymous responses** (see below). You cannot change this after publishing.
4. Press **Create questionnaire**.

On the **Questions & settings** tab, fill in the **Name**, an optional **Description**, and your questions. The question types are the same as on application forms: see [Questions](/dashboard/applications/forms#questions). **Pick one** and **Pick several** work well for votes.
Press **Save draft** to keep your work without making it public.

## Named or anonymous

| | Named responses | Anonymous responses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Players sign in with Discord | Yes | Yes |
| One response per Discord account | Yes | Yes |
| You see who answered | Yes, their Discord username | No, shown as "Anonymous player" |
| Submission time is stored | Yes | No |

Players still sign in for anonymous questionnaires, but only so each account can answer once. The answers are stored without their identity.

:::tip
In an anonymous questionnaire, avoid questions that make players identify themselves, like "What is your character's name?". The questionnaire cannot hide what they type into the answer.
:::

## Publish and open

Press **Publish questionnaire**. The dashboard asks **Publish this questionnaire?**; press **Publish and open**. Players can respond right away.

:::warning[Publishing locks the questions]
After publishing, you cannot change the questions or switch between named and anonymous. That way everyone answers the same questionnaire. To ask something different, create a new questionnaire.
:::

Once it is published you have one button at the top:

- **Close responses** stops new responses. The questionnaire stays on your portal page, marked as closed.
- **Reopen responses** lets players answer again.

## Share it

The **Share** tab has the questionnaire's link, in the form `https://apply.qbox.re/<your-slug>/questionnaires/<questionnaire-address>`. Post it in your Discord.
Players can also find it on your [portal page](/dashboard/applications/portal) under **Have your say**, with whether it is anonymous or named.

## What players see

1. They open the link and see whether their response is anonymous or shows their username.
2. They press **Sign in with Discord to respond**.
3. They answer and press **Submit response**. The page reminds them: "Your response is final once submitted."

Each Discord account can respond once. Players never see the results.

## Results

The **Results** tab shows how many responses came in, a tally for every **Pick one** and **Pick several** question, and every response below it, with the player's username for named questionnaires. Press **Refresh** to load new responses.

Results are only visible in the dashboard, to people with **Applications** set to **Manage**. Nothing about the results is shown on the portal.

## Troubleshooting

**Publish questionnaire is greyed out.**
The questionnaire has no questions players can answer. Section headings alone are not enough.

**A player gets "You have already responded to this questionnaire".**
Each Discord account can respond once, and responses cannot be changed after they are sent.

**Players see "This questionnaire is closed. It is no longer accepting responses."**
You pressed **Close responses**. Press **Reopen responses** to accept answers again.

**Players see "Questionnaire not found".**
It has not been published yet, or the link is wrong. Copy the link again from the **Share** tab.
