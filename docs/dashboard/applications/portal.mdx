# Portal page

Your organization gets its own public page at `apply.qbox.re/<your-slug>`, where `<your-slug>` is your organization's slug, the same one you see in your dashboard links.
It lists your open forms and questionnaires, and it is where players apply and follow their applications. You do not have to host anything.

## Style your page

Go to **Applications** → **Portal page**. The public link is at the top, with a copy button and **Open** to view the page in a new tab. A live preview next to the settings shows your changes as you make them.
Press **Save changes** at the bottom when you are done, or **Discard** to undo them. Changing the page needs **Applications** set to **Manage**; anyone else can look but not change anything.

The page uses your organization's name and logo at the top. Everything else is set here:

### Appearance

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Accent colour** | The colour of the buttons and links on your page. Pick a preset or type a hex colour |
| **Light or dark** | **Auto** follows each visitor's own device. **Dark** or **Light** forces one |

### Banner

The wide image across the top of the page. Press **Choose from media** and pick an image. It must be an image you uploaded to this organization's **Media** first; links to images elsewhere are not accepted. See [CDN](/dashboard/cdn).

### About your server

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Tagline** | One short line under your server name, up to 120 characters. For example "Serious roleplay since 2019" |
| **About** | What players read before they apply: who you are, how you play, what you expect. Plain text up to 2000 characters; your line breaks are kept |

### Links

Shown as buttons under your server name. Leave any of them empty to hide it.

| Link | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Discord invite** | Must be a `discord.gg` or `discord.com/invite` link. Also shown to players when nothing is open |
| **Website** | Any `https://` link |
| **Store** | Any `https://` link, for example your Tebex store |
| **Rules** | Any `https://` link to your rules |

### Visibility

| Setting | On | Off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Listed** | Google and other search engines may show your page in their results | The link still works for anyone you give it to, but search engines are asked to leave it out |
| **Show forms that open later** | Forms with an opening time in the future are listed under **Opening soon**, so players know when to come back | They only appear once they open |

### Custom domain

The **Custom domain** card is marked as coming soon. Your page stays on `apply.qbox.re` for now.

## What players see

### Your page

At `apply.qbox.re/<your-slug>` players see your banner, name, tagline, links and about text, then:

- **Have your say**: your [questionnaires](/dashboard/applications/questionnaires), if you have any.
- **Open now**: forms they can apply to right now.
- **Opening soon**: open forms whose opening time has not arrived yet, so players know when to come back. Only shown when **Show forms that open later** is on.
- **Recently closed**: forms whose closing time passed in the last two weeks.

If nothing is open, they see "No applications are open right now." and a **Join our Discord** button if you set a Discord invite.

Draft and closed forms are not listed. A form's questions are only shown while it is open.

### Applying

1. The player opens your form's link, or picks the form on your page.
2. Signed out, they see **Sign in to apply**, a **Sign in with Discord to apply** button, and a read-only list of the questions under **What this form asks**, so they can prepare.
3. They sign in with their Discord account. Their Discord account is how you know who they are.
4. They answer the questions and press **Send application**. A quick captcha check may appear first.
5. They land on their application page with "Your application is in."

### When a player cannot apply

Instead of the form, the player sees one of these, with an explanation:

| Title | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Applications are not open yet** | The opening time has not arrived. They see when it opens |
| **Applications have closed** | The closing time has passed |
| **Every spot is taken** | The form reached its **Maximum applications** |
| **You already applied** | They have an application waiting for a decision. A **See your application** button takes them to it |
| **You are already in** | You already accepted them on this form |
| **Not just yet** | They were denied or withdrew recently, and the form's waiting period is not over. They see the date they can apply again |
| **Join (your server) on Discord first** | The form requires Discord server membership and they are not in your server. After joining, they press **I joined, check again** |

### Following an application

Signed-in players find everything they sent under **My applications** in the account menu at the top of the page. Each application page shows:

- **Progress**: Submitted, In review, Interview, Decision. The page updates itself while it is open.
- **Interview note**, if you moved them to interview and wrote one.
- The deny reason, if you wrote one.
- **Messages**: the conversation with your staff. They can reply while the application is open. They are told on Discord when your staff send them a message.
- **Your answers**: what they sent.
- **Withdraw application**, while the application is still open. This closes it for good, and the form's waiting period applies before they can apply again.

## Troubleshooting

**"Pick an image from this organization's media."**
The banner must come from your organization's own **Media**. Upload the image there first, then pick it with **Choose from media**.

**"Use a discord.gg or discord.com/invite link."**
The **Discord invite** field only takes Discord invite links. Create an invite in Discord that does not expire and paste it here.

**The page warns that the portal link does not work.**
Your organization's web address (its slug) is one the portal uses for itself, for example `api` or `applications`, so your page cannot be shown there. The slug cannot be changed from the dashboard, so ask for help in the support channels of the [Qbox Discord](https://discord.gg/qbox).

**Players see "This form is not open".**
The form is a draft, closed, or paused by your plan, or its web address changed. Check the form's status on its **Share** tab.

**"Join (your server) on Discord first" even though the player joined.**
Make sure they signed in with the same Discord account they joined with, then have them press **I joined, check again**.

**"The captcha check did not pass. Give it a second and send again."**
The player's browser did not finish the check in time. Waiting a moment and pressing **Send application** again fixes it. Their answers are not lost.

**"This application is closed, so messages no longer go through."**
The player tried to reply after the application was decided or withdrawn. Messages only work while it is open.
